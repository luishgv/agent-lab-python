Soc Ops turns small talk into real conversation. Fill your board by finding people who match each prompt.

How to play

  1. 1
    Spot a match

    Scan your board for a prompt that fits someone nearby.

  2. 2
    Start a conversation

    Walk up, ask, and mark the square once it's confirmed.

  3. 3
    Claim your line

    Five matches in a row, across or diagonal, wins.
